Does Kanso-Ops replace ERP procurement or a Source-to-Pay suite?

No. Kanso-Ops works as an operational layer around existing systems. It makes reminders, disputes, supplier risks, missing documents and action plans visible, owned and measurable.

Which P2P or S2P workflows can be automated first?

The best first workflows are simple, recurring and measurable: supplier acknowledgements, document reminders, dispute follow-up, quotes in progress, delays, procurement action plans and productivity dashboards.

Why talk about procurement productivity?

Because much of the ROI comes from giving teams back time: less information hunting, fewer manual reminders, less reconstructed reporting and clearer shared decisions.
