Logistics
Unified cockpit for a multi-client co-packer
A logistics specialist in packaging manages about twenty clients. Each with their own Excel file, their own formats, their own habits.

The problem

Sales Administration juggles 20 Excel files for 3 distinct stock types (components, finished products, work in progress). No automatic reconciliation. Anomalies — stockouts, forgotten leftovers, yield gaps — are detected too late, sometimes after delivery. Sales Admin spends a full day per week on this.

Kanso's answer

The Extractor automatically parses the 20 files and normalizes the formats. The Comparator reconciles the 3 data silos in real-time. The Lookout predicts stockouts 5 days before they occur. A single cockpit replaces the back-and-forth between files — without changing anyone's habits.

~8h / week
per sales admin
i.e. ~€47,000/year for a team of 3 (based on €42/h loaded cost)
Extractor Comparator Lookout
Pharmaceutical
Multi-site supply chain for a CDMO
A pharmaceutical contract manufacturer operates 5 production sites in France. SAP is in place, but teams spend their time manually compiling cross-site data.

The problem

Each site has its own schedules, stock levels and GMP constraints. The consolidated view only exists in PowerPoint presentations updated once a week. The supply chain team spends hours preparing each steering meeting. Critical alerts (supplier delays, compliance gaps) arrive too late.

Kanso's answer

The Extractor connects above SAP and consolidates data from all 5 sites without modifying the source system. The Guardian continuously verifies GMP compliance and AI Act obligations. The Dispatcher tracks critical deadlines (certifications, audits, deliveries). A single cockpit provides the consolidated view in real-time.

~12h / week
per supply chain member
i.e. ~€95,000/year for a team of 4 (based on €42/h loaded cost)
Extractor Guardian Dispatcher
Real estate
Tenant screening and fraud detection
A real estate agency receives dozens of rental application files per week. Each file is manually verified, document by document.

The problem

A manager spends an average of 45 minutes per file verifying each document: pay stubs, tax notices, ID, guarantees. Inconsistencies (reported income vs. pay stubs, forged documents) are sometimes detected after lease signing. The resulting defaults cost money and take months to resolve.

Kanso's answer

The Extractor automatically captures and structures each document in the file. The Guardian cross-checks the data (income vs. pay stubs, employer consistency, document validity) and assigns a confidence score in seconds. The manager focuses on customer relationships and flagged files — instead of verifying everything by hand.

~35 min / file
per property manager (45 → 10 min)
i.e. ~€51,000/year for 3 managers (15 files/week)
Extractor Guardian
Procurement
Supplier panel monitoring and enrichment
A procurement team manages a panel of over 150 suppliers. Supplier files are rarely up to date, and every RFQ starts with a race against the clock.

The problem

Supplier files (certifications, financial health, contacts, capabilities) live in an Excel no one maintains. Before each RFQ, a buyer spends several days manually verifying and enriching each file. At-risk suppliers (deregistered, restructured) are only detected after the fact.

Kanso's answer

The Lookout continuously monitors public sources (business registers, ISO certifications, industry news). The Extractor automatically enriches supplier files — over 90% of data is updated without human intervention. The Comparator scores and ranks suppliers by weighted criteria, ready for the RFQ.

~3 days / month
per buyer
i.e. ~€36,000/year for a team of 3 (based on €42/h loaded cost)
Lookout Extractor Comparator
Invoicing
Automatic detection of supplier invoice discrepancies
Every time an invoice arrives, the accounting team must manually compare the invoiced prices to the contract prices. On hundreds of line items, discrepancies go unnoticed.

The problem

Invoices arrive as PDFs, reference prices live in another file (or in SAP), and the comparison is done line-by-line by hand. Verification time is considerable and undetected discrepancies accumulate month after month. Without tooling, it's impossible to know if you're overpaying.

Kanso's answer

The Extractor captures invoice data via intelligent OCR. The Comparator automatically cross-checks each invoiced line against reference prices in contracts or purchase orders. The Dispatcher triggers disputes for each detected discrepancy and tracks their resolution to closure.

~6h / week
per accountant
i.e. ~€35,000/year for a team of 3 (based on €42/h loaded cost)
Extractor Comparator Dispatcher
