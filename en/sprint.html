Frequently asked questions

The questions we always get

What if the Blueprint finds nothing?
Then you leave with proof your teams are well-organised. The Blueprint remains your property. No commitment beyond that.
Does the Harvest really pay for itself?
Yes. We only charge a share of the cash actually recovered. If we find nothing, you pay nothing beyond the Blueprint. Details are calibrated on a project-by-project basis.
Does my data leave my premises?
Never. Isolated instance per client, hosted in France. Your documents stay in your SharePoint. Our agents read, compare and flag — without ever copying your data elsewhere.
What happens after the Sprint?
The Sentinel subscription continues monitoring. You can add modules (Supplier Watchtower, AI Scout, etc.) or stop at any time. No long-term commitment.
Does it work with SAP / our ERP?
Kanso plugs on top of your existing systems — not inside them. We read your Excel files, PDFs, SharePoint. No modifications to your IS.
How long until we're operational?
15 days for the diagnosis once connected to your data, 30 for initial results. No 6-month scoping phase, no 12-person steering committee. One point of contact, one cockpit, results.
