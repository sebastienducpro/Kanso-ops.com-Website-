The Kanso product strategy

One profitable loop
before a large rollout

We do not sell a large rollout. We choose a simple risk, activate an action loop, prove ROI, then expand only if the value is clear.

01 — Choose a simple risk

A visible, frequent and measurable case

Certificates, order acknowledgements, critical stock, disputes, quality or quotes: we start from a precise topic, not a cross-functional program.

Small scope · clear value
02 — Connect what already exists

ERP, Excel and email stay in place

We capture useful signals without a major replacement. The cockpit adds the missing visibility needed to act.

No over-engineered system
03 — Activate the loop

Detect, assign, follow up, close

Every risk has an owner, a deadline, evidence and a next action. The gain is measured continuously.

Action · proof · ROI
04 — Expand if ROI holds

The risk library grows with the client

Once the first loop is useful, we replicate it to the next nearby risk: stock, contracts, disputes, quality, quotes or action plans.
